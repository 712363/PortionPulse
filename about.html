About Portion Pulse

Portion Pulse is a simple and modern web app designed to help users plan meals, control portions, and build healthier habits.

🎯 Purpose

The goal of Portion Pulse is to make healthy eating easier and less overwhelming by providing simple tools for everyday use.

👥 Target Audience

This website is aimed at students, busy individuals, and anyone who wants to improve their eating habits without complex diet plans.

⚙️ Features

Portion Pulse includes a meal planner, healthy tips, and a daily checklist to help users stay organised and consistent.

Portion Pulse was designed and developed by my Team as part of a web development assignment, focusing on clean UI design and usability.
