Healthy Tips

Simple habits that help you stay energised, focused, and consistent.

🥗 Balanced Meals

Try to include protein, carbohydrates, and vegetables in every meal to maintain steady energy levels throughout the day.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drinking enough water improves focus, digestion, and overall health. Aim for 6–8 glasses a day.

⏰ Eat Regularly

Skipping meals can lead to low energy and overeating later. Try to eat at consistent times.

🍎 Healthy Snacks

Choose fruit, nuts, or yoghurt instead of sugary snacks to keep your energy stable.

🛌 Get Enough Sleep

Good sleep supports metabolism and decision-making, helping you make healthier food choices.

📋 Plan Ahead

Planning meals in advance saves time and helps avoid unhealthy last-minute food choices.
